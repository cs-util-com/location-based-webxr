Prototype A - states
About this variant 3D covered: -

Direction A, command bar and settings drawer. One slim app bar holds what a session touches every time; one drawer holds everything else in folded sections. The bar and every panel overlay the views, so nothing on the HUD changes the size of the 3D canvas.

Visible by default: the bar (place, category, cells, Show Quests, sun time, status, Settings), the map with zoom, locate, AR and attribution, the legal link. The legend appears at the bottom of the 3D view while cells are on. On desktop the bar spans the whole window, so long place names fit and there is room for a future control; Leaflet's zoom sits just below it. On a phone in portrait it takes two rows, in landscape one.

Folded: Analysis, World, City look, Sun and light, View and Debug, all in the Settings drawer, each section showing a one-line summary of its state while folded; the full status line behind the status button; the sun slider and the quest time picker in small popovers under their buttons. The drawer remembers the section used last in this session and nothing across reloads.

Async states stay visible: a city rebuild shows "Applying..." on its row, on its section header and on the Settings button itself, so closing the drawer never hides it. Cells loading dims the cells pill on the bar. Show Quests reads "Finding..." and then carries its "340 m SW" inside the button, at a fixed width. The status button carries a lamp: green when current, amber while fetching or when the rules fell back to a snapshot, red on a failure. Below threshold is disabled with "needs cells" instead of hidden, so nothing shifts.

One panel slot: the drawer, the cell details and the light settings share the right edge of the 3D view on desktop, the map sheet on a phone in portrait (a second detent reaches up to the bar) and the right side in landscape. Only one is ever open, and in portrait the 3D view stays in sight while a look setting is changed. The light settings opened from the drawer get a back arrow to it.

e2e helpers it would need: openSettings(section) for everything in the drawer (it opens the drawer and the named section), openSunPopover for the quick slider, openStatus for the status card. Every input keeps its id and its current lowercase accessible name; the bar controls need no helper.

Measured 3D coverage (share of the visible 3D view under HUD, resting / settings open): 1280x720 0.072 / 0.536; 1280x800 0.065 / 0.533; 390x844 0.216 / 0.216; 360x640 0.284 / 0.284; 844x390 0.245 / 0.639. On a phone in portrait the drawer covers the map sheet, not the 3D view.

Weaknesses: the bar takes two rows on a phone in portrait, a fifth to more than a quarter of the 3D band before anything is opened. The drawer is one long list: a control inside it is two taps away every time (drawer, then section), and the sun time slider exists twice (popover and drawer). While a panel is open on a phone it covers the map with its locate and AR buttons. On desktop the drawer takes half of the 3D view, and in landscape the 3D band is so short that any panel covers most of it.
