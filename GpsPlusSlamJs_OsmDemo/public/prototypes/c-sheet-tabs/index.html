About this variant

Direction C, tabs on the sheet. The top header is gone. The map pane (left half on desktop, the bottom sheet on a phone, a right panel in phone landscape) gets a tab strip: Map, Analysis, World, Look, Sun, Info. A settings tab replaces the map for a moment; the 3D view keeps its full size and shows only a two-control strip (category, cells).

Visible by default: the 3D view with the strip, the sheet on its Map tab (place, Show Quests, zoom, locate, AR), the footer with the legal link and the attribution. Folded: nothing; the other tabs are simply not selected.

Async states: a rebuild disables its control and says "Applying..." beside it; its tab carries a pulsing dot while you are on another tab. Cells loading shows on the strip switch itself and on the Analysis tab. "Finding..." stays on the Show Quests button and a dot on the Map tab. A problem in the status puts a red dot on Info; errors also go to the toast.

Light dialog: lives inside the Sun tab, so it no longer covers the 3D view or the header. Key l opens the Sun tab with it expanded; Escape closes it, then returns to the Map tab.

Details: over the right of the 3D view on desktop as today; inside the sheet on phones (any tab closes it).

e2e helpers it needs: one openTab(page, name) for Analysis, World, Look, Sun and Info (Map is the default), plus openLight(page). #category and #layer-cells stay on the 3D strip with no helper. #status keeps its text inside the Info tab, so text reads work with the tab closed. The colour scheme becomes a radio group of swatches: a chooseColourScheme helper replaces selectOption. Header geometry tests are replaced by one layout test of the tabs, the strip and the footer.

Measure: hudCoverage() returns the share of the visible 3D band (the 3D view minus the part under the sheet) covered by HUD chrome. The sheet itself is not counted: it is the map's own surface in every state, and in this variant settings never add anything over the 3D band. Dragging the sheet up on a phone shrinks the band instead.

Weaknesses: on desktop the map disappears while a settings tab is open, so a map-and-settings comparison takes tab switches; on a phone AR and locate are one tab away from any settings tab; a small phone gives a settings page only about 170 px unless the sheet is dragged up; the strip duplicates category and cells with the Analysis tab (two views of one state); the colour swatches cost an e2e helper rewrite.
