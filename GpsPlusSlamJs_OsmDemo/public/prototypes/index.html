OSM demo HUD prototypes

Three independent, throwaway directions for the controls around the 3D view and the map. None of them is wired to the app; the 3D view and the map are placeholders. Open each on a phone and on a desktop.

  • A - Command bar and settings drawer

    One slim bar for the everyday controls, everything else in one drawer with folded sections.

  • B - Tool rail with topic cards

    A labelled rail of six topics; each opens one short card over the map, so the 3D view stays clear.

  • C - Tabs on the map sheet

    No header: settings are tabs that briefly replace the map, and the 3D view never changes size.

Each prototype has a "States" switcher (or ?state=name) and a folded notes panel with what it measures.
