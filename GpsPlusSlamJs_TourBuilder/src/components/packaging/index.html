Tour packaging & QR code

The authoring-side export step. packTour bundles a tour (the sample fixture, or your own tour.json) + its asset files into an uncompressed ZIP (store mode, so component 6 can range-read a single asset without decompressing); buildTourUrl + generateQr turn the hosted ZIP's URL into a scannable viewing-mode link.
