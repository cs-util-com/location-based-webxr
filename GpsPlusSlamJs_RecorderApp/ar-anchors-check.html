WebXR anchors check

Does a WebXR anchor stay on its physical spot when ARCore's world jumps? Android Chrome with ARCore only. Nothing is uploaded or stored; the log exists only until you copy it.

  1. Mark a spot on the floor (tape, a coin) and press Start AR.
  2. Aim at the mark until the white ring sits on it, then tap the screen: a green box (the anchor) and a red ring (a plain marker, not anchored) appear there.
  3. Walk an indoor loop of at least 50 m, several laps. If no jump is listed after a few laps, cover the camera for a few seconds.
  4. Keep going until 2 or more jumps are listed, then return to the mark and note whether the green box still sits on it.
  5. Press Copy log and paste the log.
