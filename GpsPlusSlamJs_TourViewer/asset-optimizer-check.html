Asset optimizer check

Runs the editor's own picture, sound and 3D model optimizers on a file you choose, or on one of the sample files, at the chosen setting and one step either side, so the results can be seen and heard next to the original. The chosen setting has a blue frame. Everything stays in this browser. The sample recordings and photo are in the public domain or under CC0; their sources are listed in playwright-tests/fixtures/asset-optimizer/SOURCES.md.

Pictures

Chosen: longest side 1600 px, WebP at quality 0.75. Either side: quality 0.6 and 0.9, and sides 1280 and 2048 px. Zoom into the pictures to compare detail; SSIM (1 is identical) compares each with the original scaled to the same size.

Sounds

Chosen: speech (a mono sound) as Opus at 24 kbps, music (stereo) at 64 kbps. Either side: 16 and 32 kbps for speech, 48 and 96 kbps for music. Listen with headphones; decoded, the length must stay the same to the sample (a player shows an Opus file about 7 ms longer: its pre-skip).

3D models

Chosen: textures at most 1024 px (colour as JPEG 0.85, data maps as JPEG 0.92 or kept PNG), 16-bit positions, 8-bit normals, 16-bit texture coordinates. Either side: 512 and 2048 px textures, and 8-bit positions with a 1 % error budget. Each result is drawn by three.js from the same camera as the original; SSIM (1 is identical) compares the two drawings.
