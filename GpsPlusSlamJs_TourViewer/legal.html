Privacy

In short: this app has no accounts, sets no cookies, and has no analytics and no advertising. The camera, your location and the motion sensors are used on your phone to place the tour; their data is not sent anywhere by the app. To show a tour, your phone downloads the tour's file, and that download is a normal web request that the servers involved can see.

What happens when you open a tour

  • This website is served by Cloudflare. Like every web server it receives your IP address, the page requested, the time and your browser's type. Requests to the app's file service (used for tours stored on Google Drive) are logged by Cloudflare, including the file identifier of the tour, and kept for [N] days to find faults. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running and securing the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • The tour's file is downloaded from wherever its creator stored it (for example Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or GitHub). That service receives your IP address and the address of this website. For Google Drive the download passes through this website's file service, which forwards only the file identifier to Google. The creator chose the service; its own privacy policy applies.
  • Creators only: the editor and the map after finishing a tour load map images from OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation), which then receives your IP address and the map area shown.

What stays on your phone or computer

  • Up to five opened tours, so a revisit opens faster. The app keeps no offline copy of itself, so opening it still needs a connection, and a tour whose file still carries its creator's walk is kept only in part. Creators can clear them under "Other settings"; anyone can clear them in the browser's site settings.
  • A small record per tour link, per printed code and per tour series: who signed it, and when this device first and last saw it - so a changed tour can be noticed. Together these records are a history of the tours opened on this device. They never leave it.
  • For creators: how far the setup got for each tour link, the last tour link and the hosting service chosen, the text of the printed poster and the tour name you typed (per tour link), and which on-screen panels you folded.
  • For creators: unsaved changes (positions, photos, the walk you recorded), until you save or delete them.

This is stored only because you asked the app to do what it needs (strictly necessary storage), never to track you.

Camera, location and sensors.

The camera image is processed on your phone to show the tour and to read printed codes; creators can take photos for their tour. Your location and the phone's motion sensors place the tour where you stand. The app does not send any of this anywhere.

When you create a tour.

Everything you place, photograph or record stays on your device until you save the tour file and put it somewhere yourself. A tour file holds your pins, photos and the positions of your codes. It can also hold the walk you recorded (your GPS track with times, a rough 3D shape of the surroundings and the pictures the camera took by itself along the way): the phone and the desktop editor leave it out of the tour file they save unless you tick the switch that keeps it, but the desktop editor always keeps it in the file you opened, so if that file is the one you host (for example in a synced Google Drive folder), the walk is public too. "Move the walk out" in the editor's Done dialog takes it out of the tour file. Photos are saved as new images [EXIF: to be confirmed].
