Make your own tour

Put stories where they happened.

A tour can be as simple as a short walk with notes and photos - with more to come. Visitors scan the code you printed and hung, and your tour opens right in their phone's browser, with nothing to install. Tours run on Android phones with Chrome.

  • Lives in your own storage
  • No app to install
  • Keeps working without signal once a tour has loaded
  • No sign-up, no account

Six steps, then your walk.

  1. Where will your tour live?

    Your tour is one small zip file in your own storage. Visitors' phones load it from there; once it has loaded, it keeps working without signal.

      Already have a tour zip on this device?

    1. Paste your tour's share link

      Paste the share link to your tour zip. Keep the file where it is: your printed codes point to it.

      Your posters' codes carry this link, with your link wrapped inside - it is what a phone opens when it scans a poster:

      A shortened link makes the printed code coarser and easier to scan from afar. Not every shortener works: some, bit.ly among them, open for you but stop the tour loading for visitors, so opening the short link yourself proves nothing. Paste the short link here and wait for the tour to be found before you print.

    2. Print your posters

      Print the PDF at 100 % (actual size) - each page has a 10 cm bar to check it.

      On the poster
      Posters to print
      Settings
      Try this tour as a visitor on this phone

    3. Hang it where the tour starts

      Fix the printed code where the tour starts: flat on a wall or a post, at about chest height, in a spot a phone can film squarely from a metre away. Visitors scan it to open the tour, and again in AR to be placed exactly.

    Questions about this tour? Contact its maker:

    Create it on site, in AR

    Now the tour itself: stand at the hung code, start creating, and walk the route, placing text pins and photos where they belong. When you are done, the tour file is saved to this phone, and the last step shows how to put it in place.

    This step runs on site and needs an Android phone with Chrome that supports AR: open this page there. Here, the editor opens a tour you made: fix its texts, move its pins, remove photos.

    Open the editor

    The recording holds the tour link, your GPS track and the 3D shape of the surroundings.

    In AR: point the camera at the hung code until it is measured, walk the route placing pins and photos, then tap Done - the tour is saved.
