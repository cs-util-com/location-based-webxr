# .dependency-cruiser.cjs

## Purpose

[dependency-cruiser](https://github.com/sverweij/dependency-cruiser)
configuration for the Tour Viewer: the two universal structural rules, plus
two import bans of its own.

## Rules

- `not-to-unresolvable` (error) — fail on any import that cannot be resolved
  (catches path typos and missing files).
- `no-circular` (error) — fail on any circular dependency (the "component
  dependency loops" guard).
- `viewer-not-to-editor` (error) - the visitor page (`src/*.ts`) never
  imports the desktop editor (`src/editor/`), which loads Leaflet statically
  (M6 E1 plan review #5).
- `document-rules-through-tour-document` (error) - the shared rules of the
  tour a creator edits (`object-edits.ts`, `finish-rebuild.ts`,
  `tour-content.ts`, and `authoring-draft.ts` for `hostedObjects` and
  `applyObjectChanges` - the r875 milestone review #6; a rule is per
  module, so the draft's other rules are protected with them) are imported
  only by the tour document (`tour-document*.ts`, D29-D31), the shared pure
  modules themselves and tests. Every other importer is named in the rule with its reason:
  - permanent, read-only helpers: `viewer-placement.ts` (the visitor's photo
    spots), `creator-placement.ts` (the AR placement's world view);
  - permanent, the draft's own modules: `draft-persistence.ts` (its storage)
    and `editor/editor-draft.ts` (the desktop's draft store and its offer
    rules), and its peer `creator-draft.ts` (the phone's draft store; it
    reads the tour only through the document);
  - `editor/edit-main.ts` is NOT named: it reads the saved file's name
    through the session (`savedName()`), so the page imports none of the
    rules;
  - TEMPORARY: `creator-finish.ts` (the phone's Finish: its rebuild I/O),
    until it saves through the document's save too. The phone's tour
    itself is the document since its adoption (`creator-document.ts`),
    which removed `creator-settle.ts` and `creator-stations.ts` (it reads
    the document's `manifestToWrite`) and made `creator-draft.ts`
    permanent. Why the Finish is not on the save yet:
    `tour-document-save.ts.md` ("Not here yet").
  - Removed when the desktop's save became the document's thunk
    (`tour-document-save.ts`): `editor/editor-session.ts` (it saves
    through the thunk and shapes its edits by the document's
    `pinTextChange` / `objectMoveChange`) and `object-editing.ts` (the
    phone's pin text, through `pinTextChange`). The rule passes without
    them, and a probe import of `object-edits.ts` from `object-editing.ts`
    was reported again.
  - Verified when added by probe imports from `src/` and `src/editor/`,
    both reported; the `authoring-draft.ts` widening reported exactly its
    two unnamed importers (`draft-persistence.ts`, `editor/editor-draft.ts`)
    before they were named.
  - The TEMPORARY entries today: `creator-finish.ts`. No check fails on one that
    is no longer needed (declined in the review: an adoption removes its
    own line); this list is what to re-read after one.

## Usage

```bash
pnpm run check:boundaries   # depcruise -c config/.dependency-cruiser.cjs src
```

`check:cycles` (dpdm) additionally guards cycles from the `src/main.ts` entry;
the two tools overlap on circular detection by design (defence in depth).

## Tests

No unit test; exercised by the `check:boundaries` gate inside `pnpm test`.
