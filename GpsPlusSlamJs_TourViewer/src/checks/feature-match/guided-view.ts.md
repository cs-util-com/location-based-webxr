# guided-view.ts

## Purpose

The guided view of `feature-match-check.html`, the page's default (`?ui=cockpit` shows the cockpit view instead, `cockpit-view.ts`). It walks the tester through the run plan one pair at a time: an intro, the run screen (step card, viewfinder, countdown, the two photo slots and one big button), the pairs sheet, the end screen with Download all and the summary, and an optional measure sheet. Ported from the chosen prototype `public/prototypes/feature-match-2-guided/` (markup in the page, styles in `guided-view.css`).

## Public API

- `startGuidedView({ root, fm, engine, camera, log, fail })`: wires the view found under `root` and returns its render, which the entry calls on every store change. Throws `feature-match-check: missing <selector>` when the page lacks an element it needs.
- `guidedVideo(root)`: the view's video element, for the page camera.
- The camera start, the countdown, the photo, the pair and run zips, the single files and the summary are `plan-io.ts`, shared with the cockpit view; the summary goes into the run zip as `feature-match-summary.txt` (`SUMMARY_FILE`).

## Invariants and assumptions

- **State in the store.** Everything a screen shows comes from `guided-model.ts` (`runView`, `pairRows`, `endView`, `planPairOf`, `runPairs`) on the page state; taps only dispatch actions (`plan*`, `guided*`, `runZip*`). Photos go in through `put`, in the same step as `planPhotoTaken`; a refused photo leaves nothing behind.
- **Outside the store**, as browser resources or transient UI nobody else reads: the screen (intro, run, end) and the sheet shown, the motion level and whether a reading was seen, the ghost toggle (the countdown's timer, the AudioContext, whether the phone moved during the countdown, the camera start's generation counter and the device line are in `plan-io.ts`), the pair grid's last rows, and which frame each canvas last drew (`data-id`).
- **Intro text.** The hands-off line follows the plan's countdown and sound settings (`introCountdownText`): "... taken 5 seconds after your tap, with a beep per second", or, with the countdown off, that each photo is taken at the tap.
- **First screen.** The intro when the plan has no photo yet (`plan.shots === 0`), else the run screen. The intro's Start reads "Continue where you left off" once a photo exists; the light-test place buttons (1-3) are off once any slot holds a photo.
- **Camera.** The run screen starts the camera unless a run is busy or the last start failed (`state.cameraStart` is `failed`); a start that leaving the screen stopped (the help opened while the permission prompt was up) does not block the next one, which starts again on return; the intro, the end screen and the flat targets stop it. With the camera off, the big button's Capture only starts it, and a chip in the finder says "starting camera..." while a start waits (the permission prompt), "camera unavailable: tap Capture to retry" after a failed one, else "camera off: tap Capture".
- **Photos not stored.** While a photo could not be written to the phone's database (`state.photoStoreFailing`), the run screen shows the chip "not stored on this phone: download soon" and the end screen a line saying a reload would lose them.
- **Countdown.** A capture tap vibrates (30 ms, only at the tap) and, with a countdown of 3 or 5 s, sets `guidedCountSet({ shot, left })`, beeps (660 Hz) and ticks once a second; at 1 it clears the count and takes the photo. Countdown off shoots at once. A shot with no camera picture yet, or a photo the reducer refuses (the page turned busy), says so in a short note at the foot of the finder ("No photo: the camera had no picture yet. Tap again.", "No photo A: the page was busy. Tap again.", a framework toast, `#g-shot-note`); the next photo taken clears it. Cancel, opening the pairs sheet and leaving the run screen cancel it (logged `countdown: <why>`); a tick the reducer refuses cancels it as `refused`. The timer stops whenever the state's count is null (a reset or a move). The photo beeps (1320 Hz) and flashes for 360 ms; sound follows the plan's setting.
- **Photo log line.** `photo A: <slot> #<seq> WxH grey mean M`, with " (phone moved during countdown)" when the steady hint read moving at any point of the countdown; a complete light pair adds `light check <slot>: <result>`.
- **Steady hint.** `devicemotion` rotation rates feed `motionStep`; the chip shows "steady" or "phone moving" (`isMoving`, threshold `STEADY_THRESHOLD`) only on the run screen, once a reading was seen, and not while the pair is reviewed.
- **Ghost and review.** While photo B is due, "Show A" lays photo A at 40 % over the finder; it turns off after a new photo A and on every move. With both photos taken, the finder shows them side by side with the light-check or completion note; tapping a slot toggles its retake (`guidedRetakeToggled`).
- **Big button.** Cancel, Capture/Retake A or B, Next pair (`planMoved`, logged `next pair: <slot>`), Next missing pair, or Finish (the end screen), as `runView` says.
- **Pairs sheet.** Every slot with its state; a tap moves there and shows the run screen. Countdown (3, 5, off) and the sound switch change the plan's settings. "Add an extra pair (other)" adds one (up to `MAX_EXTRA`) and moves to it; "Finish and download" opens the end screen; "Start over" asks first and then resets the plan (refused while busy). Escape closes either sheet.
- **Download all.** `runZipStarted` (refused unless idle with a complete pair), then every complete pair (`runPairs`) as its two PNGs and its frames JSON under the pair's own names, plus `feature-match-summary.txt` and the log, packed with `packFilesAsZip` and saved with `downloadBlob` under `runZipName(session)`. Saved: `planRunDownloaded`, then "Saved <name> (<size>). Send this file." The save dialog closed: "Not saved: the save dialog was closed. Nothing was lost." and no download counted. A failure (a frame not loaded, an encode error): an `ERROR download:` line and "Could not prepare the zip: ... Nothing was lost; try again." The button reads "Preparing the zip..." and is off meanwhile.
- **Summary and log.** The end screen shows `summaryText` (session, pairs complete, missing, light checks, retakes, downloads, the phone line from `deviceSummary`); Copy summary and Copy log write to the clipboard ("copied", or "copy refused: select the text and copy by hand").
- **Measure sheet.** Holds the measuring block, moved in at start with its own listeners: the synthetic run button, Options, Speed test and Results (table, pictures, report, log; the Options note is hidden). "Run on pair <slot>" (the label names the current pair) runs the benchmark on the current plan pair only (`engine.startRun("plan")`, the photo-test settings; methods and sizes from Options); its state line is the run's status only while `planRun` is that pair (`measureText`), else whether the pair has both photos. The current pair's single files (photo A, photo B, sizes and device, matches after a run on this take) save through `downloadBlob`, one at a time with the page's busy `saving` and `state.fileSave` ("preparing...", then the note), like Download all; a saved file marks the take downloaded (`planFileKept`, `planFilesDownloaded`, `planKeptDropped`), so a retake gets a new running number. The saved Blob is not kept in the side table: the take's photos already are. "Flat targets" stops the camera and opens `flat-capture.ts`.

## Differences from the prototype

- **Measure.** The prototype's "Run on my pairs" ran every pair; the build's "Run on pair <slot>" runs the current pair in a full-height sheet, the one the single-file buttons save.
- **Phone on its side.** At 915x412 the prototype's step card overflows: the foot lines lie over the sequence and "then" and B are cut off below the card. The build has the same markup and fixes it in `guided-view.css` (no diagram below 560 px of height in landscape, a card that scrolls as the last resort); the layout test pins it. Below 400 px of height (800x360, a common phone on its side) the purpose line goes as well and the card column widens to 55 %. Space left below the foot line, Windows fonts / Verdana standing in for the wider Linux fonts of CI: 37 % -2 / -2 px, 46 % 28 / -2, 50 % 28 / 11, 55 % 28 / 28 (CI measured -1.8 px at 46 %); 915x412 keeps the 37 % column with 46 / 15 px to spare. Only pair 1's text is measured: a longer step text scrolls the card, as the last resort. Screenshots side by side at 915x412 confirmed both the prototype's defect and the fix.

## Example

```ts
const camera = createCameraControl(guidedVideo(document), cameraDeps);
const view = startGuidedView({ root: document, fm, engine, camera, log, fail });
fm.store.subscribe(view);
```

## Tests

- Screen rules: `guided-model.test.ts` (run view, pair rows, end view and download label, run pairs, device line, steady hint with its threshold sweep); the actions: `feature-match-slice.test.ts`.
- The view in the browser: `playwright-tests/feature-match-check.spec.js`, the guided tests (intro to end with a faked camera and countdown off, Download all through the save picker and through the link fallback, a reload that keeps the photos and gives a new photo its own id, a closed save dialog, the plan run on a pair with stubbed SIFT, the countdown on the page clock, the "starting camera..." chip, the camera starting again after the help left a start pending, a countdown ended by a refused second and a refused photo with its note, Download all packing and failing without loss, the measure table at 412 px, and the layout at four viewports, 800x360 among them). `measureText` is unit-tested in `guided-model.test.ts`.
