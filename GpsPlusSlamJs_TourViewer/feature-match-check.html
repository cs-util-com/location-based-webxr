Feature matching benchmark

Times keypoint extraction and matching of three methods on this device, on two photos you take, and draws what each one matched. Models load from the network on first use (ALIKED + LightGlue about 64 MB).

  1. 1 Take photo A

    Pick the pair, then take photo A.

    not taken

  2. 2 Take photo B

    Change only what the pair names (the light, the turn, the distance or the side step), and keep most of what A shows in view.

    not taken

  3. 3 Run

    Starts by itself after photo B, with every method and size ticked in the options; the camera is off meanwhile. The pictures under Results fill in as each one finishes. A full run takes minutes.

    waiting for photo B

  4. 4 Save

    after the run

    Every file is named by its running number and pair, and after Download all the pair moves on to the next one. Offers one zip holding everything, this page's log and report included, and each file on its own, to save on this device only: the two photos as PNG, a JSON of their sizes, grey values and this device's browser, OS version, model, cores, memory, screen and GPU, and a JSON of every method's keypoints and matches. The photos carry pixels only, no location, time or EXIF data. Nothing is uploaded.
