          How well do feature matchers work on your phone?
          
          
          
            You take pairs of photos of the same scene. Between the two photos
            of a pair you change exactly one thing: the light, a turn of the
            phone, the distance or where you stand. Later, matching programs try
            to find the same points in both photos.
          
          
        
          
        
            
          
          • 
            
              
            
            
            
              Keep the phone still while it shoots.
              A tap shakes the phone, so each photo is taken 3 seconds after
                your tap, with a beep per second. Hands off until the
                click.
            
            
          
            • 
          
          • 
            
              
            
            
            
              Nothing is uploaded.
              The photos stay on this phone until you tap Download. They hold
              pixels only: no location, no time, no camera data.
            
            
          
            • 
          
          • 
            
              
            
            
            
              You send back one file.
              At the end, one zip with all photos, each named by its pair, plus
              a short summary you can paste into a message.
            
            
          
            • 
          
          • 
            
              
            
            
            
              Nothing personal in the picture.
              No faces, papers, screens, house numbers or windows to the
              outside. Aim at something with detail: shelves, a poster, a
              patterned rug.
            
            
          
            • 
        
          
        
          
          
          
            Light tests (need a dark room) at
            
            
            
          
          
          
          
            Lock the screen rotation and do not zoom. The camera asks for
            permission once.
