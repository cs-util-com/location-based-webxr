Make a splat of a place

Turns a walk you recorded into a 3D view of the place, shown in the tour editor while you edit.

Everything happens on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.

    Good to know

    • Stays here. The recording is read on this computer and the result is saved to your disk. No file leaves it.
    • Takes a while. A walk is trained in stretches of about 30 m, a few minutes each. A whole walk takes 30 to 60 minutes.
    • Leave it running. You can use other tabs. Keep this tab open.
    • Stop any time. Finished stretches are kept, and you can carry on later.

    Make a splat

    Use a desktop computer

    Making a splat trains a 3D model on a graphics card for 30 to 60 minutes. A phone cannot do this.

    What to do

    1. Copy your recording file to a computer.
    2. Open this page there, in Chrome or Edge.
    3. Choose the recording. It stays on that computer.
