The sign is the speaker; the words stay near. The sign says only who speaks and whether the voice runs. The plate under it carries the line being spoken while you can read it. When you cannot - too far, too steep, turned away - the same words are in the box at the bottom. Never in both places.

The creator's side: choosing words on screen or on the sign →

Try

  • Tap the words on the plate to listen; tap again to pause.
  • Drag the plate's words down to hear a line again, up to skip ahead - the same gesture as in the box.
  • Step back to 16 m, or turn away: the words move to the box; come back and they return to the plate.
  • Tap the sign for the whole text.
  • View (top right) → Captions: the box always, whatever the tour chose.
