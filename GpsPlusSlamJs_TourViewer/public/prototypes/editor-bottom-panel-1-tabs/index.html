Notes: VS Code-like tabs

Idea. The bottom bar keeps only what you act on: the map credits, the selection's actions, and four labelled toggles that open a tabbed panel, as in a code editor. Each toggle carries a count only when there is something to look at.

  • Removed from the bar: "Saved to tour.zip" (the top bar's "Not saved" says it; saving now shows a toast and a "saved" mark in History), the tilt readout (now in Performance, under View), the hint text, and the Debug toggle (its three numbers became the History and Performance tabs).
  • The panel pushes the 3D view up but not the side panels. Problems and History point at stations and items; clicking a row selects the thing in the list and the inspector, so both must stay fully visible next to the panel. The map controls live in the view and move up with it. View > "Panel spans under the side panels" lets you compare the other choice.
  • Close: the same toggle, the x, or Escape. Resize: drag the top edge (drag it nearly flat to close). Maximise: double-click the tab strip or the edge. Ctrl+J shows or hides it.
  • On a phone the side panels become sheets, so the panel is always full width there; the toggles keep their icon and badge.

Try: open Problems, click a row, use its fix; open History and jump back past "Changed the circle of The old gate"; drag the map to queue tiles.
