Walk it - keys and mouse

W A S D, Up/Down
walk (hold Shift to run)
Left/Right
turn
Drag empty space
look around (bird's-eye: pan)
Right-drag
look / orbit
Double-click ground
walk there (bird's-eye: go down there)
N / P
glide to next / previous station along the walk
Tab
lift to bird's-eye and back down
Click
select a thing, a station ring or badge
Drag a thing
grab and move it across the ground
Wheel while grabbing
turn it 15 degrees (Shift: 1 degree)
Alt + drag a ring
change the station radius
G
pick the selection up and carry it, click to set down, Esc to cancel. You can walk while carrying.
Shift + arrows
nudge 0.5 m (relative to your view)
Ctrl + Shift + arrows
nudge 5 cm
Ctrl + Left/Right
turn 5 degrees
Ctrl + Up/Down
raise / lower 10 cm
F

Delete
delete the selection
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z
