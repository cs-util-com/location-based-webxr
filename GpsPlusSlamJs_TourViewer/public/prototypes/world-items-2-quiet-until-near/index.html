Drag the dot on the map to walk. Nothing needs a tap.

How a free-standing item behaves here

  • From afar: one small marker that says what kind of thing waits.
  • From 13 m: the thing itself shows, without sound.
  • At 6 m: after a moment it begins. A ring shows the moment, and a tap skips it. A film or a character waits until you look its way.
  • Walking away: from 9 m it fades, at 13 m it pauses. Come back and it continues.
  • Finished things stay quiet until tapped. They never start twice by themselves.
  • One sound at a time. What began first keeps the sound. The other waits as Next, until the first ends or you are clearly at the other one, 4 m closer to it.
  • Stop means quiet: nothing starts by itself until you have walked 8 m on.
  • The item only knows Play, Pause and Ended. Here nearness presses Play. In a story, a step would.
