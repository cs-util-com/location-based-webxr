H2 - Bottom sheet

A sheet at the bottom with big, thumb-friendly buttons. Pull it up to see how the tour is doing. Tap something you placed and the same sheet swaps to that object's actions.

  1. Wait a moment: the printed code gets measured.
  2. Drag the view to aim the blue ring. That stands in for moving your phone.
  3. Pull the sheet up by its handle (or tap the handle).
  4. Tap a pin or the photo: the sheet shows Move to cursor, Edit, Delete and a big Done.
  5. Save & finish saves everything to your tour file.

In landscape the sheet becomes a side panel. Throwaway prototype: nothing is saved; the camera is a drawing.
