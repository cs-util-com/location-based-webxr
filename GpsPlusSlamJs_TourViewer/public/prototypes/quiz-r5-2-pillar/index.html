A pillar on request. An egg is hidden at each thing an answer names; it glitters faintly. Walk close and it opens and flies into the box. From the first egg on you may choose. Stuck? Tell Mara in the box: one short pale pillar then rises at the nearest egg still hidden. Keep looking at it and the egg fills from below and comes to you; the pillar sinks, and the next one rises while help is on.

Try

  • Drag sideways on the view to turn; tap the ground to walk there (3.5 m/s); hold Walk (Fast for 3.5 m/s). On a desktop: W A S D, Q E to turn, Shift to hurry.
  • Walk to the fountain straight ahead, then choose it at once: the shortcut.
  • Tap "Mara, help me find them" and turn slowly towards the glow at the screen side.
  • The handle on the box's top edge folds it: the found eggs fly back to their places, and in again when it opens.
