Mara

-

The character guides. The three answers are hidden like eggs at the things they name and glitter faintly. Walk close to one: it opens and flies into the box, where you may choose it at once. Stuck? Tap Mara's name in the box and ask her. She then guides you in words ("a bit more to the left ... there"), the side of the box towards the egg glows in her colour, and looking that way long enough fills the egg and its empty slot together; then it flies in.

Try

  • Drag sideways on the view to turn; tap the ground to jump (near an egg you land just outside it, facing it); hold Walk (Fast for 3.5 m/s). On a desktop: W A S D, Q E to turn, Shift to hurry.
  • Tap the name Mara in the box to ask for help. "Show me" asks for a clearer hint (a pale light at the egg); "I'll look myself" ends the help.
  • The handle on the box's top edge folds it; found eggs then go back to their places and return when it opens.
