The bubble travels. A character's speech bubble is one object. While it can be read beside his head, it stays there with its tail. When its letters (or, in a story step, its choice buttons) get too small, it leaves the view, or he is farther than the open distance, it flies down, its tail shrinking into the edge, and becomes the box at the bottom. Same words, one place, never two; and only one figure's words at a time.

Try

  • Tap the words, or the baker himself, to listen. Tap again to pause.
  • Drag the words up or down to move through his voice, in the bubble and in the box.
  • "Choose": his line ends with a question; the two buttons open inside the same surface, and since they are too small to tap beside him, it flies to the box. Pick one.
  • "Two speak": the ferry keeper joins. The voice that runs owns the box; the other figure folds to its name tag. Tap a name tag to hand over.
  • "14 m", "Turn away" and back: watch the bubble travel. Drag sideways on the view to look around.

Head-on, at the middle of the view; distances from the figure. Docks from: open within 9 m / 15 m / no limit. With the bubble growing past 2.4 m its letters stay about - tall however far he is, so the letter rule docks at once or never.

    Faked here

    • The voices are soft generated tones, one note per word, on times invented from the letters. No recording.
    • The figures are drawn stand-ins, not photos of anyone. The camera, the square and the walk are simulated; nothing runs in AR. The sideways drag stands in for turning the phone.
    • The station circle is 20 m around the baker; voices play only inside it.
