The creator places the answers

Why this is in 3D: the place is the answer. The creator stands each answer where it belongs, beside the fountain or under the tree, and the visitor answers with their feet. A card with four buttons cannot say "here".

The strongest case against: the phone's position is off by up to 3.6 m for 9 readings in 10, so two answers a few metres apart blur into one, and placing four markers is the heaviest step a creator has. A card never misreads and needs no walking.

Try Edit first: drag a marker's ring, or walk and press Where I stand or Where I aim. Move the warning slider and watch the red links. Then Play, Continue, and walk into a circle. The white dot is the simulated phone. Cost, Sample, crowded puts four answers 3 m apart.
