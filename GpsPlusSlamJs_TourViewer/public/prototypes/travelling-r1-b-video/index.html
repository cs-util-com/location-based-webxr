The screen comes with you. A video's player is one object. While its picture is big enough on the screen and in view, it plays on its screen in the world. When it is not, and the video plays, the same player flies to a small corner of the phone's screen and keeps playing there; when the screen can be watched again it flies back. The bottom centre belongs to the narration box, so the player docks in its own corner beside it. One picture, one place, never two.

Try

  • Tap the picture to play or pause; the bar holds sound and captions, in the world and in the corner alike.
  • "15 m", "Turn" and "65°": the playing player docks. "3 m": it flies back.
  • "Voice box": a narration speaks at the bottom centre while the video plays in its corner. "2 videos": only the last one tapped docks.
  • Drag sideways on the view to look around.

Faked here

  • The clips are canvas drawings driven by the clip's own clock, with a soft generated tone for sound. No real footage, no recording.
  • The camera, the square and the walk are simulated; nothing runs in AR. The sideways drag on the view stands in for turning the phone.
  • The narration box is a silent stand-in for round 5's box. The station circle is 20 m around the screen.
