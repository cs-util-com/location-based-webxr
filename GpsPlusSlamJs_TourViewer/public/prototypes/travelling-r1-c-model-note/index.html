The voice of another stop is playing stand-in, silent: it owns the box while it plays

The note comes to you. A placed model carries notes. Tap a note's marker and its card opens beside the point it names, joined to it by a line. When the card cannot be read there (the point is out of view, faces away, is hidden behind the model, or the card is too small or too wide) the same card flies to the box at the bottom edge, and back when it can be read again. One card, one place, never two.

Try

  • Tap a marker on the fountain. Tap the card in the world to bring it close: in the box it has Previous, Next and "Back to its place".
  • "Next" flies the card from point to point. "Round" walks behind the fountain with the basin's note open: the card leaves for the box as the basin turns away. "Far" opens the note on the back.
  • "Voice": another stop's voice takes the box; a card that would go there folds into its marker until the voice ends.
  • Drag sideways on the view to look around.

Faked here

  • The model is a generic fountain built from simple shapes; the notes are made up.
  • The camera, the square and the walk are simulated; nothing runs in AR. The sideways drag stands in for turning the phone, and the camera turns to the open note by itself, as a visitor would.
