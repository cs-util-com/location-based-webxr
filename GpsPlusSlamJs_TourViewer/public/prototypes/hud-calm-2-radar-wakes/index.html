Stop for a moment and the radar wakes up.

The radar wakes when you stop. While you walk, the screen is yours: the radar sleeps as a faint ring. Stand still for a moment and it wakes. Outside a station it shows the stations. Inside one it shows the station's things at their true places: white is still to see, grey is seen.

What "seen" means here

  • Short text, picture: in view and near (8 m, 10 m) for 1.5 s, 2 s.
  • Model: in view within 10 m for 3 s.
  • Long text: its last page was shown (tap it to turn pages).
  • Video, sound: played to its end. Stopped part way, it stays white.
  • A character who starts a story: that story played to its end.

What the HUD holds

  • Exit, top left. The radar, top right (asleep while you walk).
  • At the foot, one thing at most: the story's step control while a story runs, or one play/pause for what is playing.
  • Everything else stands in the world: texts, captions, titles, the beam.
