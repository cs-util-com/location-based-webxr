Photo test, about 10 minutes

Three scenes, one photo at a time

Each scene starts with one base photo. Every later photo changes one thing - the light, the angle, the distance or where you stand - and is compared against that base. Measuring starts by itself as soon as a scene is complete.

  1. 1
    Light Phone stays still

    Stand the phone on a table. Four photos; you stand up only twice, to dim the light.

  2. 2
    Flat surface Hand-held

    A poster or a patterned door. Six photos: tilted, far, near, to the side. Turning the phone is not needed - it is computed.

  3. 3
    Deep Hand-held

    Aimed into a hallway. Three photos, one from the side.

  • Nothing gets lost. Each photo is saved on this phone the moment it is taken. If the page reloads, it picks up where you were.
  • Nothing personal in the picture. No faces, papers, screens or windows to the outside. Aim at something with detail.
