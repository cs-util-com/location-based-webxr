Folders, tree first

Items are grouped where the structure of a station already lives: in the station tree. The 3D view only follows: a selected folder shows one gizmo at its pivot and a light teal outline around its items.

Why this direction

  • A folder is structure, and the tree is where structure is seen: stories, items and now folders sit in one list, made and changed with one set of moves.
  • Picking rows is exact. In a crowded scene a tap in 3D hits the wrong card; a row cannot.
  • It reuses what creators already know: the grip, the nested drag and "Not possible here" of story folders, and the one gizmo of a single item.
  • Mouse and finger get the same model: Ctrl/Shift-click on a desktop, a Select mode with checkboxes on a phone (or a long press on a row).

The strongest case against it

Grouping is a spatial wish ("the two cards beside the stone"), but the tree shows names, not places. A creator must know which "Old photo" is the one beside the stone; with twenty items called "Picture", picking by name is slower than drawing a lasso around them in 3D. On a phone the list and the scene share one screen, so grouping means switching between them.

Try this

  1. Pick "Built in 1820" and "Old photo": Ctrl+click (or Shift+click a range) on a desktop; on a phone tap Select and tick both, or long-press a row.
  2. Press Group (Ctrl+G) and type a name.
  3. In 3D, drag the round arrow to turn the folder, the middle arrows to move it, a white dot to scale it.
  4. Click one card in 3D: it is picked alone and moves alone; the folder stays outlined.
  5. In the tree, drag a card's grip out of the folder, onto the station row or between free items. It keeps its place in the scene.
  6. Drag "Water sound" onto the middle of the folder row to put it in.
  7. Drag the folder onto the story: "Not possible here".
  8. Duplicate the folder (Ctrl+D), Ungroup it (Ctrl+Shift+G), then Undo (Ctrl+Z).
