Prototype notes: the docked asset shelf

Idea. The asset shelf is its own panel at the right edge, beside the inspector. Fold it with the arrow in its head (or the Assets toggle in the bottom bar): it becomes a slim rail whose rings keep showing what is still being optimized, so the shelf never has to be open to know where the files are.

  • Drop files anywhere on the page, or press Simulate drop for four sample files.
  • Use an asset at once: drag a card onto a station, a story or an empty item in the tree, or onto an item's file field; or open a card and choose Use in... (the phone's way).
  • An item whose file is still being optimized says so, and uses the original until the optimized one replaces it.
  • The video fails the first time; Retry works.
