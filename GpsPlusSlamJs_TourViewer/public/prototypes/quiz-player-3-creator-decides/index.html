What to try

Pick a run at the top, step through its beats with the arrows, or press Play and the page walks for you. Drag to look around, tap the ground to walk there (W A S D on a desktop). The quiz you see is one player: the tour's data decides whether a quiz can live in the square, and the player drops it back into the card the moment the square cannot be trusted.

Why the card, why the square (live)

    Position

    Weak stops any fill at once, so a brief wrong position never locks an answer. The answers fall back to the card only after the position stayed weak for the drop delay, and lift again only after it stayed good for the lift delay, and only while the visitor has not started answering in the card.

    Values to judge (each is an assumption)

    HUD footprint, this run

    Share of the screen covered by HUD boxes (Exit, radar, toast, card, pill, story card), the number of HUD elements and the number of overlapping pairs. Recorded about a second after each beat is reached; turn the phone to fill the other column.

    The format: what a placed quiz needs

    
      
    Everything new is optional, so every quiz written today still plays, as a card. Text, code and number answers never need a place.
    

      
    The rules this page plays by
    
      
      
        
    • The creator decides whether a quiz may be placed: a single or several-answer quiz whose every answer has a place, at least the spacing apart, and not marked "card".
      • 
        
    • The player decides whether it is placed now: only while the position is vouched for, and only until the visitor chooses the card.
      • 
        
    • One quiz, one band: while a quiz is open it owns the bottom of the screen, either as the card or as the small pill, never both and never over a story card. The walk's own layer (Exit, radar, toast) stays.
      • 
        
    • Nothing is lost in a hand-over: what was found stays found, a chosen answer stays chosen, and nothing counts without the visitor: Check in the card, or the grace ring closing while they still stand there.
      • 
        
    • Other items wait: while a quiz is open they dim in the square and their cards do not take the band. A sound already playing is not stopped.
      • 
        
    • Answer types: single goes to the square when placed. Several-answer quizzes are found in the square and chosen in the card. Text, code and number always play in the card.
      • 
      
    

      
    The strongest case against this
    
      
    Two presentations of one quiz is two things to learn. A visitor whose position flickers sees the quiz move between the square and the card, and may lose trust in both; the hysteresis hides short flickers, but a long walk under trees could still hand over several times. The creator also places answers that may, on the day, play only as a card, so the effort of placing them can be invisible to many visitors. A card-only player is simpler and always works.
