Warmer, in the world. The answers are hidden as eggs at the things they name, and each glitters faintly. Walk close to one: it opens and flies into the box. You may answer from the first egg on. If you are stuck, tap the "still hidden" slot in the box: Mara then helps through the world itself. The egg you turn towards glitters faster and brighter, its sparks climb higher, and if you keep facing it, it fills from below and comes to you.

Try

  • Drag sideways on the view to turn; tap the ground to jump there (near an egg you land just outside its find radius, facing it); hold Walk (Fast for 3.5 m/s). On a desktop: W A S D, Q E to turn, Shift to hurry.
  • Tap "still hidden" in the box to ask for help, tap it again to stop.
  • Tap a found answer in the box to choose it; tap it again before its bar runs out to take it back.
  • The handle on the box's top edge folds it: the found eggs fly back to their places, and come home when it opens.
