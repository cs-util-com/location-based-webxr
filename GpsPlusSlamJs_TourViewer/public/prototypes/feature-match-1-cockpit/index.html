Photo pairs for feature matching

You take 20 pairs of photos. In each pair ONE thing changes between photo A and photo B (the light, a turn, the distance, a step to the side). The phone measures how well three matching methods find the same points in both, and saves each pair as one file on this phone. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Put the phone on a stand. Lock the screen rotation. Do not zoom.
  • Why still? A photo taken while the phone moves is blurred, and a blurred pair measures the shake, not the method. The page waits until the phone is still and takes the photo itself, so you do not have to touch it.
  • Nothing personal in the picture: no faces, documents, screens, letters, house numbers, and no window showing the outside.
  • Aim at something with detail (shelves, pictures, patterned fabric).
  • Chrome may ask once whether this page may download several files. Choose Allow, or switch to "save all at the end" under Settings.

Tap the big button to start the camera.

    Tap a pair to take it (again). Every file is named by its running number and pair, for example feature-match-03-1b-s2-light-dimmed.zip.
