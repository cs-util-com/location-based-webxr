Preparing cloud noise...
Notes

Technique. A spherical cloud shell (bases 1.25 km, tops up to 3.05 km) is raymarched in its own low-resolution pass. Density = a baked 2D weather map (clustered cumulus domes with gaps, small clouds between) thresholding a tiling 3D Perlin-Worley shape noise, a height gradient that gives flat bases and rounded tops, and Worley erosion detail. Lighting: a short light march toward the sun (Beer), a two-lobe phase function with multiple-scattering octaves (bright backlit rims), a powder term (dark edges facing away), and a blue sky ambient on the shadow sides. Distance-proportional steps start at a blue-noise offset that changes every frame; the result is reprojected (camera and wind) and accumulated, then upsampled into a full-resolution pass that draws the sky, a Beckmann sun-glitter sea with mip-filtered sparkles and cloud shadows, two-component haze, and the sun.

Tiers.

  • low: canvas at 0.75 of CSS pixels (dpr capped at 1), clouds at 0.5 of that (one quarter of the pixels), up to 32 primary steps (about 18 across the slab), 2 light steps, no erosion detail, 2 scattering octaves, 1 shadow sample on the sea, longer history (blend 0.1).
  • high: canvas at device pixels (capped at 1.5), clouds at 0.5, up to 80 primary steps (about 48 across the slab), 5 light steps, erosion detail near the camera, 3 octaves, 2 shadow samples, blend 0.18.

  • 390x844 high: 10 km 33 / 383, 3 km 17 / 300, 30 km 383 / 450.
