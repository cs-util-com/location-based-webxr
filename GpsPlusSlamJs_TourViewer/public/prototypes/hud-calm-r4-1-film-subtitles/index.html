Film subtitles. One dark plate across the top edge carries the words being said, as roll-up captions: a new line enters at the bottom, the top line leaves. Its lower edge is the clip's timeline, and the round handle riding on it is play/pause and the thumb you drag to go back. The compass hangs below the plate's right end and marks who speaks.

Faked: there is no voice. Word timings are made up from each word's letters (about 155 words a minute), so the highlight shows the idea, not real speech.

Two switches at four levels, "radar at all" at tour and station only. Each says "as above" until it is set; the nearest level that says something wins: item over story over station over tour. Tap a switch to cycle it.

What the HUD holds

  • Top edge: exit at the left end; while something speaks, the plate with its words, its timeline and the handle; at the right end one small control at a time (show the way, Next, leave).
  • Below the plate's right end: the compass, asleep while you walk. The voice speaking now is its one coloured, breathing mark.
  • Everything else is written in the world: signs, long texts, captions, titles, the beam.
