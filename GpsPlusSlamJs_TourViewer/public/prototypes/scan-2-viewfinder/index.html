Demo controls

S2 - Viewfinder. The whole screen becomes a viewfinder: four corner brackets, a scanning line and one line of text at the bottom with a countdown to Skip. The animation plays once inside the frame, then shrinks to an icon. On detection the brackets snap onto the poster.
