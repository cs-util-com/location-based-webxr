Thumb ribbon. No plate for the words: they stand on a short dark shade from the top edge, white and shadowed. The line being said sits in the middle, the line before above it, the next below. Under the words, the play/pause button rides on a bar: drag it back to hear a moment again, or pull the words themselves down. Under that, the compass, in the look the tour's creator chose; a coloured notch marks who speaks.

Compass look (tour)

The creator's choice, one look at a time; no visitor switch. Each switch below says "as above" until set; the nearest level wins: item over story over station over tour.

Faked here

  • Every word's time is invented from its letters (about 150 words a minute, pauses after commas and full stops). A real tour needs timings from the voice or a subtitle file.
  • The camera, the town, GPS and walking are simulated; nothing here runs in AR.
