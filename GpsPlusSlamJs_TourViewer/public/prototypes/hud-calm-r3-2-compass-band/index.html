Walk up to the orange beam.
Drag sideways to turn, drag up to walk (or W A S D).
Stop for a moment and the band at the top wakes up.

Compass band. A thin band at the top shows what is around you by its direction, like a game compass: the middle is straight ahead. Near things are larger and brighter, at a straight-line scale; things beyond the range are faint rings at their true direction. It sleeps while you walk and wakes when you stop. Spoken words and subtitles run in one line under it, beside a small play/pause.

Author settings (what the tour's author chose)

Each switch says "as above" until set. The page starts on the preset "Starting example": the story "Water for all" is off the band and Greta, who starts it, is on it; "Every switch as above" clears that. The nearest level wins: item over story over station over tour. "Radar" exists on the tour and stations only. The band and the hint obey at once.

What "seen" means here

  • Short text, picture: in view and near (8 m, 10 m) for 1.5 s, 2 s.
  • Model: in view within 10 m for 3 s.
  • Long text: its last page was shown (tap it to turn pages).
  • Video, sound: played to its end. Stopped part way, it stays white.
  • A character who starts a story: that story played to its end.
  • A place a story sends you to (the rim stone, the bell piece): reached while that story runs.

What the HUD holds

  • Exit, top left. The band, top centre (asleep while you walk).
  • Under the band, the caption line, only while something speaks or a story runs: play/pause (or Next), who speaks, the words, and in a story the hint and leave.
  • Everything else stands in the world: signs, long texts, titles, the beam.
