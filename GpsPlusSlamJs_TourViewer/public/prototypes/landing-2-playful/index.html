Hi! Liked that tour?

You just walked a tour. Make your own.

Hang a little code where your tour starts, then walk around and drop pins and photos right into the world. People scan the code and follow along. All you need is a phone, a printer and a nice spot.

Let's make one How it works
  • Your own storageThe tour lives in your Drive, Dropbox or server. No server of ours in between.
  • No app to installVisitors scan with their camera. It opens in the browser.
  • Works offlineOnce loaded, the tour runs in the browser. Weak signal on site is fine.
  • No accountFree, no sign-up. Just a link and a printed code.

Six little steps

Pinny here will walk you through it. Most of it happens outside, with your phone.

  1. 1Pick a home

    Choose where the tour file lives: your Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, GitHub or own server.

  2. 2Paste the link

    Copy the share link and paste it here. We check it can be reached.

  3. 3Print the code

    One click makes a PDF. One code per poster, printed at actual size.

  4. 4Hang it up

    Flat on a wall, at chest height, right where the tour begins.

  5. 5Create in AR

    Scan your code, walk around, place text pins and photos in 3D.

  6. 6Swap the file

    Your tour is saved on the phone. Replace the hosted file, and the code keeps working.
