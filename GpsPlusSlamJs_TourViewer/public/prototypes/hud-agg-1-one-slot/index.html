HUD -
Drag to turn, drag up to walk, tap things in the square. The arrows at the top step through the demo; the label in between opens it.

One slot

The screen owns exactly one "now" slot at the foot and one nudge line above it. Every item of the station, the story and the walk ask for the screen with a rank; one arbiter gives the slot to the top request and the nudge line to the best one-liner. Everything else waits silently. There are no edge marks, no item list, no second panel.

Ranking rule

  1. What you tapped yourself (a pin's text, a picture, a model's card). Your own choice wins; what it covered comes back when you close it.
  2. A story step, a quiz included. While it is on screen, free items show only in the world: their glow and their moving bars say they are playing.
  3. What is playing. If two play, the one you started last has the slot and the sound; the other plays on, muted, and the nudge line offers a swap.
  4. A start where you stand ("you are there - Play"). It never starts by itself: a tap is always needed.
  5. Nudge line only: position weak, fresh walk news, "walk closer", the one thing out of view that matters, where the walk goes next.

Inside the station, and not

Items of the station you are in are awake: their own colour, a glowing ring, a kind badge. Items of a station you are not at are asleep: grey, no ring, no badge (look ahead to the well). The radar shows the station you are in as a filled disc around you; other stations are dots on its rim.

A seventh and an eighth item

They join the queue. The HUD does not grow: still one slot and one line. They show in the world only, until they rank first. Try "Items: 8" in the demo panel and watch the queue (the queue is a demo readout, never shown to a visitor).

What changes from today's walk

  • The walk sheet's peek becomes the nudge line ("Next: The well - 55 m ahead"). The station list opens in the slot from a tap on the radar.
  • The focus edge pill and the station edge arrows are gone; the toast is the nudge line.
  • The narration card, the video card, the captions strip and the story card are one panel that changes content and never stacks.

The strongest case against it

One slot means one thing at a time, and a queue the visitor cannot see. With two things playing and a story waiting, the visitor has no way to know what is queued except by walking and tapping in the world; a swipe can hide the only control of something still playing. Without edge marks, an item out of view is found only if it is the one the nudge line names, so a quiet item in a corner may never be found. And text is slow to read in sunlight while walking: the nudge line asks to be read, where a glyph would only ask to be seen.

One slot. One panel at the foot, one line above it, one arbiter deciding who gets them.
