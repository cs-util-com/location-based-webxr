Words over the circle. A narration circle has no sign: you stand inside it. The spoken words are one object. While they can be read where they stand over the circle, they stay there; when you turn away, look down, stand too close or see them too flat, they fly down and dock at the bottom edge, and fly back when they can be read again. One copy, never two. Leaving the circle hides them and stops the voice.

Try

  • "Walk in": nothing shows outside the circle; inside, the words appear where they can be read, or docked. Tap the words to listen.
  • Tap the words (over the circle or in the box) to pause or play. Drag them up or down to move through the voice.
  • "Turn round" and "Look down" in the centre: the angle, not the distance, moves the words. Compare the three places below.
  • "Leave": the words hide and the voice stops; walk back in and it carries on. Drag on the view to look around.

Faked here

  • The voice is a soft generated tone, one note per word, on times invented from the letters. No recording.
  • The camera, the square and the walk are simulated; nothing runs in AR. In AR a finger turns no camera; the drag on the view stands in for turning and tilting the phone.
