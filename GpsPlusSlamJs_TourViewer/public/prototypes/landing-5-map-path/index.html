Prototype L5 - Spatial path

Make your own tour

Put stories where they happened.

A tour is a short walk with notes and photos floating in the real world. Visitors scan a printed code and it opens in their phone's browser, right where they stand.

  • Lives in your own storage
  • No server of ours
  • Runs offline once opened
  • No app to install
Six stations. About 15 minutes, then your walk.
