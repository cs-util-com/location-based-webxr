What this is

The reading sign speaks. While the plate under it can be read, the spoken words run there; when the sign leaves the view or gets too small, the same words go on in the box at the bottom. Never in both places.

Tap the plate or the box to pause or play; drag the words up or down to go back or ahead. A drag off the plate turns the view (in AR the phone does that).
