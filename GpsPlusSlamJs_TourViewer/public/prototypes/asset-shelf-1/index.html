Idea. The right panel gets two tabs: Selection (the inspector) and Assets (the shelf). Files dropped anywhere land on the shelf and are optimized in the background; the tab and the bottom bar show the progress.

  • Use while processing: drag a card (by its grip on touch) onto a station, a story or a media item in the list, onto an item's file field, or press its Use button.
  • Pin as a section: the split button in the shelf's head puts the shelf under the selection, so both show at once (not on a phone).
  • On a phone the panel is the details sheet; the list's shelf button opens it on Assets.
