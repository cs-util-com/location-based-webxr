The character throws. Mara asks; the answers leave from her name in the box with a trail in her colour, land as short light pillars at the things they name, and the question rides on them. Walk to one: it wakes, comes to eye level, fills from below and opens. Tap the ground to jump, drag to look, hold Walk or Back (or W A S D) to move fast.

Values to judge

Tap fallback and retry in this variant

  • The answers in the box are tappable only when the position is weak, or after 20 s of standing still. Otherwise the box says to walk.
  • A wrong answer gets no retry: Mara says the right one and the story goes on.
