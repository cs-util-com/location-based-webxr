Tour Viewer design prototypes

Design drafts made to choose a direction (polish round M1), kept as an inspiration gallery. They are not connected to the app; buttons only switch states. The Tour Viewer took L5 (the setup page), H1 (the authoring HUD, with an action strip) and S1 (the scan overlay); the others are here for anyone who would rather build one of them.

The entry and setup page (for a new creator)

  • L1 - EditorialA calm magazine-like scroll story with large illustrations.
  • L2 - Playful illustratedBright, friendly, a little mascot walks through the steps.
  • L3 - Dark, AR-forwardA dark page whose hero shows AR content appearing over a scene.
  • L4 - App-like wizardOne step per screen, a progress bar, like a native app.
  • L5 - Spatial pathThe steps are stations along a tour route on an illustrated map.

Creating the tour in AR (the authoring HUD)

  • H1 - Floating chipsMinimal chips at the edge; a chip row next to a selected object.
  • H2 - Bottom sheetLarge thumb-friendly buttons in a sheet that changes with the mode.
  • H3 - Drag along the surfaceTap to select, then drag the object along the surface.

Starting a tour as a visitor (the scan overlay)

  • S1 - Centre cardA small translucent card with a phone moving over the code.
  • S2 - ViewfinderCorner brackets frame the view and snap onto the poster.
  • S3 - Coach markAlmost nothing on screen: a pulsing target and a short hint.

The visitor's start page (after scanning a poster)

Made 2026-10-12, awaiting a verdict. A small "Prototype state" control in a corner switches between the page's states: loading the tour, location not yet allowed, location denied, ready, checking AR, AR not supported, and a tour that could not be loaded.

  • V1 - The poster's continuationThe paper look of the printed poster, come alive: the tour's name, "AR tour starts here", one big Start.
  • V2 - Dark, AR-forwardA night scene with the tour's pins already appearing over it, like a game or a camera app.
  • V3 - Three stepsAllow location, start AR, point at the poster - each step ticked off as you go.
  • V4 - Minimal, one tapThe tour's name, one big button in the thumb zone, the details folded away.
