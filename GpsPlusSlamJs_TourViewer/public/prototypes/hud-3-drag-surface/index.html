H3 - Drag along the surface

Direct handling. Tap a pin or the photo to select it, then drag it with your finger: it slides along the ground and the detected surface lights up under it. A small radial menu around the object holds Move to cursor, Edit and Delete.

  1. Wait a moment: the printed code gets measured.
  2. Drag the empty view to aim the blue ring. That stands in for moving your phone.
  3. Place a pin (round button) or take a photo (shutter).
  4. Tap the "Welcome!" pin, then drag it. Or use the radial buttons around it. Done leaves it.
  5. Finish tour saves everything to your tour file.
