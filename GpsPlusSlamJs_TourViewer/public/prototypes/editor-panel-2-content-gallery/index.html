About this prototype: the content gallery

Adding content is visual. A station's panel has an Add to this station gallery: one tile per kind of content, with a small drawing of what a visitor will see and one line about it. Picking a tile asks where it should stand (click in the view), or drops it at the station. A story is made the same way, as a folder, and is then filled from the story's own gallery.

  • Radius sits with the position under "Place and reach": both answer where the station is and how close visitors must come, and the ring is drawn in the view while you change it. The top of the panel is kept for what visitors read: name, description, picture.
  • Adding lives in the panel of what you add to: the station's gallery adds to the station, a story's gallery adds steps to the story. "+ Add content" in an open list row leads to the same gallery.
  • Destroying lives only in the "Danger zone" at the very end of each panel, far from the gallery. A station or story asks once, with its item count, and can be undone. Everyday actions (Duplicate, Copy position) are in the bottom bar.
  • The panel follows the selection: station things, story things or item things (Put on the ground, Align to station, Move to another station), never mixed.
