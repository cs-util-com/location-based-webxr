About this prototype: status items with their own drawers

The bottom bar is a quiet status bar. Each status item appears only while it has something to say (map tiles loading, problems, steps to undo, slow frames) and opens its own small drawer that rises from it. Pin a drawer and it becomes a floating mini-window over the 3D view; several can sit side by side.

  • Removed from the bar: the "Saved to" text (the top bar's "Not saved" mark plus a short note after saving), the tilt readout (the view and the compass show it), the hint line (belongs in Help) and the Debug toggle (its numbers now live in the History and Performance drawers, in readable size).
  • Never hidden for good: a quiet item can still be opened from View > Status, or from the "..." at the bar's right end, where each item can be set to always show.
  • Drawers: one opens at a time; Escape or a click outside closes it; drag its top edge to make it taller. Pinned windows stay until closed: drag them by the header, resize them at the corner.
  • The bar's layout: map credits at the left; the selection's actions (Duplicate, Align to station, Copy position) at the centre-right, only while something is selected; the status area has a fixed width, so the actions do not jump when items come and go.
  • Phone: items shrink to icon and number, drawers become full-width sheets, no pinning.
  • The side panels are static stand-ins here; they only show what a problem row selects.

Try: wait for the street map's "server busy" countdown; open "4 problems" and click a row or a fix; open History and jump back two steps (a fixed problem returns); drag the view to load new tiles; pin two drawers.
