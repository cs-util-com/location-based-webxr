Classic disc. A round radar at true scale: everything inside its range sits where it is, everything beyond waits on the rim in its true direction. It sleeps while you walk and wakes when you stop. At the foot, one small pill: play/pause, who speaks, and the words being said.

Author settings (the visitor's page obeys them live)

Two switches at four levels, "radar at all" at tour and station only. Each says "as above" until it is set; the nearest level that says something wins: item over story over station over tour. Tap a switch to cycle it.

URL: ?author=b-off,lion-off,well-nohint,radar-b-off

What "seen" means here

  • Short text, picture: in view and near (8 m, 10 m) for 1.5 s, 2 s.
  • Model: in view within 10 m for 3 s.
  • Long text: its last page was shown (tap it to turn pages).
  • Video, sound: played to its end. Stopped part way, it stays white.
  • A character who starts a story: that story played to its end.

What the HUD holds

  • Exit, top left. The radar, top right (asleep while you walk).
  • At the foot, one pill at most: play/pause, the speaker's dot, the words being said now. During a story it also carries Next and leave.
  • Everything written stands in the world: signs, long texts, captions, titles, the beam.
