Greta
Tap to hear Greta. Then: tap the box to pause or play, drag the words up or down to go back or ahead.
-

Scroll box. The words sit in one box at the bottom edge: dark, slightly see-through, left-aligned, at most three lines. Drag the words up or down and the narration moves with them; tap the box to pause or play. Words turn white as they are spoken. When the story asks something, the question and its answers come in the same box.

Try

  • Let Greta speak; tap the box to pause, tap again to go on.
  • Drag the words down to hear a sentence again, up to skip ahead.
  • At the end, "Next" asks a question in the same box; answer it.
  • Drag sideways on the view to look around; the compass at the top follows.

Faked here

  • The voice is the browser's own text-to-speech, one sentence at a time. Where the browser reports each spoken word, the highlight follows it; where it does not, the highlight follows times invented from the letters and waits for the voice at the end of each sentence.
  • The narration starts with a tap. In the app it starts only inside the station's circle; the tap is a shortcut of this page.
  • The camera, the square and Greta are simulated; nothing runs in AR.
