One box that travels. The narration box is one object. While the sign can be read and is in view, the box hangs under it as a plate in the world. When it cannot be read, it flies down and docks at the bottom edge; when the sign can be read again it flies back up. Same words, one place, never two.

Try

  • "Walk up": the words wait in the box until the sign is close enough, then fly up under it. Tap the words to listen.
  • Tap the words (on the plate or in the box) to pause or play. Drag them up or down to move through the voice.
  • "Turn away" and back, "16 m" and "3 m": watch the box travel. Drag sideways on the view to look around.
  • Tap the sign to open the whole text; tap "AI voice" to see who speaks.
