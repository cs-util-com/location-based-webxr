Tour Viewer

Make your own AR tour

You just walked one. Building your own takes a few short steps, right here in the browser.

What a tour is

Text pins and photos that sit at real spots. Visitors scan a code and see them through their phone camera.

It stays yours

Your tour is one file in your own cloud storage. No account here, no app to install, and once loaded it runs without good internet.

How it works

Paste a link, print a code, hang it up. Then walk around and place things in AR. Visitors just scan.

    Step 3

    Print your codes

    Each poster gets its own code. All of them open your tour.

    Step 4

    Hang a code where the tour starts

    Visitors start here, and so do you when you create.

    • Flat on a wall or boardNot wrapped around a pole or bent.
    • Chest heightAbout 1.3 m from the ground, easy to scan.
    • Good light, kept dryA clear sleeve helps outdoors. Leave room to step back.
    Step 5

    Build the tour in AR

    Take your phone to the code you just hung up.

    • 1
      Stand at the code and scan itThis ties your tour to the spot, so content lands in the right place.
    • 2
      Walk the routeYour phone keeps track of where you are.
    • 3
      Tap to place text pins and photosPut them right where they belong.

    Not at the spot yet? Open this page again when you are standing at the code.
