What to look at

  • While the visitor looks, only a slip stays at the thumb. No answers are on screen until they tap Answer.
  • Walking is the game, so walking away pauses nothing. The riddle stays open and can be answered anywhere.
  • A wrong answer sends them back to look. It does not offer the next guess at once.
  • A missed riddle marks the spot in the world instead of naming the answer. That also works for a typed answer, which the tour file does not hold in clear.
  • Typed answers are really judged here by a salted hash. Try patience in any case.
