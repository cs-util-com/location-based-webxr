One by one. The question comes in the box at the bottom. Then the box folds to one line and the answers fly out one at a time, each to the thing it names. An answer whose place is out of view waits: the line says which way to turn, and it flies once you look there. Walk to an answer: it wakes, comes to eye level and fills; stay and it opens; step away to take it back.

Try

  • Drag sideways on the view to turn; tap the ground to jump near an answer; hold Walk (Fast for 3.5 m/s). On a desktop: W A S D, Q E to turn, Shift to hurry.
  • Tap the handle on the box's top edge (or drag it down or up) to fold or open the box: the answers fly out or in each time.
  • Tap an answer in the world: close by it starts listening, far away it says "Walk closer".
  • With the box open, tap an answer there to choose it without walking.
