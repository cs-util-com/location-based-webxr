Technique. One full-screen shader, analytic per pixel, on a spherical Earth (radius 6371 km), so every term could become a patch on a globe's sea and sky shaders. Sea: the wind-driven slope variance of a Cox-Munk distribution is split by pixel footprint. Wave octaves the pixel resolves are drawn as moving slopes (sharp sparkles near the camera); smaller ones add their variance to the glint lobe, so the sun path becomes a broad smooth sheen with distance. Fresnel-weighted sky reflection. Haze: air plus a low marine haze layer, Chapman optical depth, single scattering with sun transmittance and ozone, which gives the bright horizon band, warm toward the sun. Clouds: one deck at 1.3 km, a 2D coverage field relief-marched as a height field (flat base, domed tops), with wrap light, sky light, a backlit rim and shadows on the sea and in the haze below the deck.

  • Low tier: 0.6 of CSS pixels (steps down to 0.4 while p95 is above 40 ms), 5 wave octaves, 5 relief steps plus 2 refinements, 4 cloud octaves, no cloud self-shadow, no lens ghost.
  • High tier: device pixels (at most 2x), 8 wave octaves, 14 relief steps plus 4 refinements, 5 cloud octaves, a self-shadow sample toward the sun, faint lens ghost.
  • Measured headless with software WebGL, a forced-sync render, all three views, ranges over repeated runs (only the ratios mean anything): high 1440x900 2.7-6.3 s, low 1440x900 0.47-0.80 s, high 390x844 0.9-1.9 s, low 390x844 0.18-0.32 s. Low is about 5-6 times cheaper than high. Live on this device: see the readout.

Self-check against the reference. The clouds are the weak part. They are height-field domes: no volume, no overhangs, no light through thin parts, hard and slightly dithered rims, and at 3 km the grazing march leaves combed streaks on the tops. Fix: a raymarched volume over a 2D weather map, about 3-6 times this cost on a phone. The far deck is sparser than the reference's. No crepuscular rays through cloud gaps (shadowed in-scatter, about 8 extra samples per pixel), no cloud reflections in the sea, no real bloom (the halo is analytic). The sea is slightly too blue-cyan at 10 km. Far glitter has no single sparkles, by design: they are below pixel size there.
