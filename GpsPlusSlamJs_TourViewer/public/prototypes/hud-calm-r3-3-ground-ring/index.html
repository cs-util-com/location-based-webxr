The ground ring. The radar lies on the ground a little ahead of you, as a thin ring, and you see it when you look down. It is a true map: one metre on the ring is always the same many metres around you, and anything beyond its range waits on the rim in its true direction. On the screen there is only Exit and one line at the foot: who speaks or what plays, the words, and play/pause at its end.

Author settings

What the tour's author decides. Every level says "as above" unless it is set; the nearest level wins: item over story over station over tour. Tap a switch to cycle as above / yes / no. The HUD obeys at once.

What "seen" means here

  • Short text, picture: in view and near (8 m, 10 m) for 1.5 s, 2 s.
  • Model: in view within 10 m for 3 s.
  • Long text: its last page was shown (tap it to turn pages).
  • Video, sound: played to its end. Stopped part way, it stays white.
  • A story's later step (the rim stone, the oven, the bell): its words were spoken there.
  • A character who starts a story: that story played to its end.
