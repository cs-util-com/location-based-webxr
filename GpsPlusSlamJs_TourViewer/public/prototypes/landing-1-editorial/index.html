Tour Viewer - for creators

Leave a story where it happened.

Make a tour people open by scanning a poster. They hold up their phone, and your notes and photos appear right where you placed them.

  • Free
  • No account
  • About an hour, start to finish
Begin

A few quiet things worth knowing.

  • It stays yoursYour tour is one file in your own storage - Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, GitHub or your own server.
  • Nothing of ours in betweenThere is no server of ours holding your tour. The page just reads your file.
  • Fine with poor signalOnce a tour is opened it runs in the browser, even offline. No good internet needed on site.
  • No app to installNot for you, not for your visitors. A phone camera and a browser are enough.

The whole way

Six steps, start to finish

You can do the first three at your desk. The last three happen outside, where your tour begins.

  2. Paste the link1 min
  3. Print the posters5 min
  4. Hang one up10 min
  5. Create in ARas long as you like

01Step one

Where will your tour live?

Your tour is a single file. Pick a place you already use to keep files - it only needs to give out a link that anyone can open.

Not sure? Google Drive is the most common choice.

Google Drive

    I have my link

    The starter is a tiny, empty tour. You will fill it in AR later.

    02Step two

    Paste the link

    Paste the share link you just copied. This link is your tour's address - the printed code points to it, and it never has to change.

    We take a quick look in the background. If the file is not there yet, that is fine - you can upload it later.

    Waiting for a link.Paste it and we check it right away.
    03Step three

    Print your posters

    One code per poster. Most tours need just one, where the tour begins. Print a few more if people may start from different places, or as spares.

    Bigger codes scan from further away. 16 cm suits a poster at arm's length.

    cm

    In the print dialog choose 100 % or Actual size, not "Fit to page". Then check with a ruler: the code should measure 16 cm.
    04Step four

    Hang it where the tour begins

    The poster is more than a door into the tour. It is also the fixed point everything is placed around, so where and how you hang it matters a little.

    • Flat against a wall or a board, not angled or curled.
    • At chest height, where a phone naturally points.
    • Somewhere it can stay - if it moves, the tour moves with it.
    • Good light, no glare from glass or a glossy sleeve.
    05Step five

    Create the tour in AR

    This is the good part. Stand at the poster with your phone, tap Start creating and scan the code. From then on, the camera knows where you are.

    Walk the route. Where something happened, tap to leave a text pin. Where a picture was taken, place the photo. Everything stays exactly where you put it - for you, and later for every visitor.

    Tip: walk the route once without placing anything, so you know your stops.

    06Step six

    Done - put the tour back

    When you finish, your phone saves the updated tour as a zip file. Replace the file you uploaded in step one with it. That is all.

    • Same link
    • Same printed code
    • Your finished tour
