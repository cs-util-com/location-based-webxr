Wayfinding HUD demo

Edge arrows point at off-screen targets, rings mark on-screen ones, and everything hides when you arrive — with a hysteresis deadband so nothing flickers. On a phone this runs in AR with tap-to-place waypoints; on the desktop you walk a simulated scene.

Desktop simulator: W/A/S/D or arrow keys to walk, drag to look around.
