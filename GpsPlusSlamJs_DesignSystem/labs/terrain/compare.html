Terrain colour approaches, judged along the fly-in

The terrain lab's fly-in onto the Alps, captured at 300, 100, 30 and 10 km (style C, the far field itself, where its blend acts: 1500 to 300 km) at a day and a low sun, one row per colour approach (globe round-5 plan, §3.3). The numbers are logged, not judged: the owner picks. globe-albedo is the provisional default. The globe's pixel is modelled (its imagery under its sun, no veil or specular), which favours the imagery styles: see compare.js.md.
