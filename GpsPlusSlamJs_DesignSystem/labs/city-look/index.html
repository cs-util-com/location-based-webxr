City look

Throwaway prototypes of how the Osm library's buildings could look, on real data: a Lucerne suburb with hardly any tags, Heidelberg's old town with many, and two New York areas, Midtown's towers and Park Slope's flat-roofed rows. Each variant opens next to today behind a divider. Heat colours on the pages use stand-in scores.

  • V1 faithful: Lucerne, Heidelberg, Midtown, Park Slope
  • V2 closed roofscape: Lucerne, Heidelberg, Midtown, Park Slope
  • V3 architect's model: Lucerne, Heidelberg, Midtown, Park Slope
  • V4 phone and AR: Lucerne, Heidelberg, Midtown, Park Slope
