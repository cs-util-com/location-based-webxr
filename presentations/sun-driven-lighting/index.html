Alissa - 1:05. Use the documented first Sun Position plan as the example. Codex assumed clockwise azimuth from North. That sounded reasonable, but comparing the plan with SunCalc exposed the mismatch: its zero is South, with positive angles toward West.

The issue was caught in plan review, before the implementation was accepted. We revised the plan/conversion and added tests for all four cardinal directions.
